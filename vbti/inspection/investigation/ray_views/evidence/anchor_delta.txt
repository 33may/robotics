face ray is anchored on the box WALL, which this change does not move. Only

DOWN and lose a little, while v=0 cells and the flat LAN block gain.

closer -> farther (+0.013) and cell 9,0 farther -> closer (-0.005) — and
they cancel: the count stays 9/11. Both sit within 5 mm of the old
endpoint, which is the band where "closer" and "farther" are a coin toss
rather than a finding. Recorded because a reader comparing this table to
bench_summary.txt row by row will meet them.

sampler puts the clear majority of executed views closer to the surface
than the run actually got — holds on all four runs under the anchor the
machine actually uses.
